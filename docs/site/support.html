Timbre

Support

Timbre is a tuner and music-lessons app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

Get help

Email azerish25@gmail.com with your device, iOS version and what happened, or open an issue at github.com/azerish25-ux/tuner.

Common questions

The tuner doesn't hear me. Check that Timbre may use the microphone: iOS Settings → Timbre → Microphone. Play one string at a time, close to the phone, in a quiet room.

Restore Timbre Pro. On the subscription screen or in Settings, tap Restore purchases. Subscriptions belong to your Apple Account.

Cancel or change a subscription. iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Timbre Pro.

Turn off anonymous usage analytics. In the app's Settings, turn off sharing usage data. See the privacy policy.

Delete your practice history. In the app's Settings → Clear practice history. Deleting the app removes all its data from the device.

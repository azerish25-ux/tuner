Timbre Support

Timbre is a tuner and music-lessons app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch.

Get help

Email shahroodazerov@gmail.com with your device, operating-system version and a description of what happened. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

The tuner does not hear me

Allow microphone access in your device's Settings for Timbre. On recent iOS versions, app settings are under Settings → Apps → Timbre. Play one string at a time near the microphone, in a quiet room. Bluetooth input can have different latency from the built-in microphone.

Restore Timbre Pro

Open Timbre's Settings or subscription screen and choose Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Subscriptions and payment details are managed by Apple.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Open your device's Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions → Timbre Pro. Removing Timbre does not cancel an active subscription. See Apple's subscription help.

Your songs and recordings

Charts and audio files you import stay on your device. Delete an imported song in Your songs to remove its local library entry and imported files. Keep your own backup of any original recording. Optional Apple Music searches and playback use Apple's service and authorization separately.

Privacy controls

In Timbre's Settings, turn off Share anonymous usage data to stop new analytics uploads and discard queued events. You can clear practice history in Settings. Read the privacy policy for local storage, optional services and website hosting information.

Accessibility

Timbre follows your device's text and accessibility preferences. To report an accessibility problem, include the screen, device, setting used and steps needed to reproduce it. Feature validation is ongoing; this page does not certify that every task works with every assistive technology.

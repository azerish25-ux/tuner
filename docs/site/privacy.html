Privacy policy

Microphone audio stays on your device

Timbre listens to your instrument or voice to measure pitch. Analysis happens on your iPhone or iPad, in memory. Timbre does not record, store or upload microphone audio. You can stop listening in the app or revoke microphone access in iOS Settings at any time.

What Timbre stores

On your device only: your settings, custom tunings, the instruments you choose, and summaries of practice sessions (date, instrument, tuning, duration and measured accuracy). Home-screen widgets read your streak from storage shared between the app and its widgets on the same device. Clear the history any time in Settings → Clear practice history. Deleting the app deletes this data; device backups follow your backup settings.

Anonymous usage analytics

To learn which parts of Timbre help people play (for example, how many finish setting up, open a lesson or start a trial), Timbre sends anonymous product events to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. An event names what happened in the app ("lesson finished", "paywall shown", "trial started", the plan and its price), the app version, the device's system version and language. For a subscription, events also say what the App Store reports about it afterwards: that it renewed, was refunded, or was set not to renew, with the plan's name and the kind of offer it used (for example a free trial).

  • Events are not linked to you. There is no name, email, account, advertising identifier or location. Each installation has a random identifier, scrambled on the device before it is sent, which cannot be traced back to you or to your Apple Account.
  • Events are never used for tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and never sold.
  • Events never contain audio, recordings or your song charts, and nothing you type, with one exception you choose to send: Request a song on the Songs tab sends the title you type (trimmed, at most 80 characters) and the instrument filter that was on, so the songs people ask for most can be counted. It is sent like any other event, not linked to you, and not at all while sharing is off (below). Please don't put personal information in a request.
  • Timbre also sends Apple's daily performance and stability summaries for the app (launch time, responsiveness, memory, and counts of crashes or hangs), from Apple's MetricKit, so problems can be found and fixed.
  • If you installed Timbre after tapping an Apple Ads advertisement, Timbre asks Apple which campaign it was (Apple's AdServices attribution) once, and sends the campaign, ad group and keyword numbers with the events. This needs no tracking permission and identifies the advertisement, not you.

You can turn sharing off at any time in Settings in the app; from then on events stay in the device's own log.

Settings Timbre downloads

Timbre may download a small settings file from its developer's website (for example, which version of the subscription screen to show, or which songs are free this week). The request carries no identifier and nothing about you.

To show the app's App Store rating on the subscription screen, Timbre may ask Apple's public App Store lookup service for it, at most once a day. The request names the app and the country of your App Store, and nothing about you.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. Timbre receives only Apple's verified record of what you own, never your payment details. Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. See Apple's App Store privacy notice.

Notifications

Notifications are created and delivered on your device; nothing about them is sent anywhere.

  • Practice reminders, if you turn them on: at the time you choose, on days you haven't played yet, naming your next lesson and your streak, and a weekly summary on Sundays.
  • Trial reminder, if you start a free trial and allow notifications: two days before the trial ends, with how much you have practised during it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, Timbre reports scores, your practice streak and badges to Apple's Game Center under your own Game Center account, so you can see leaderboards and challenge friends. If you agree to it, Timbre reads your Game Center friends' practice streaks to show where yours ranks; it shows only a rank and a count, never a friend's name, and keeps them only while the app is open. Timbre keeps no player ID, name or friend list. Game Center is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing

When you share a card (a lesson result, your week), it is a picture of that achievement and a link to Timbre on the App Store or its website. Nothing is shared unless you choose to.

Children

Timbre does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

# Timbre for iOS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026. Published at https://azerish25-ux.github.io/tuner/privacy.html.

Timbre is provided by **Shahrad Azeri Sharghi**. Questions: [azerish25@gmail.com](mailto:azerish25@gmail.com).

## Microphone audio stays on your device

Timbre listens to your instrument or voice to measure pitch. Analysis happens on your iPhone or iPad, in memory. Timbre does not record, store or upload microphone audio. You can stop listening in the app or revoke microphone access in iOS Settings at any time.

## What Timbre stores

On your device only: your settings, custom tunings, the instruments you choose, and summaries of practice sessions (date, instrument, tuning, duration and measured accuracy). Home-screen widgets read your streak from storage shared between the app and its widgets on the same device. Clear the history any time in **Settings → Clear practice history**. Deleting the app deletes this data; device backups follow your backup settings.

## Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. Timbre receives only Apple's verified record of what you own, never your payment details. Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. See Apple's [App Store privacy notice](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-store/).

## Notifications

If you start a free trial and allow notifications, Timbre schedules a local reminder before the trial ends. It is created and delivered on your device.

## Analytics and advertising

This version has no advertising and no third-party analytics. Product events stay in the device's log. If anonymous usage analytics are added, this policy and the App Store privacy label will be updated first. Timbre does not sell data.

## Children

Timbre does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

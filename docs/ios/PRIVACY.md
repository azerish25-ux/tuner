# Timbre for iOS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Timbre is provided by **Shahrad Azeri Sharghi**. For privacy questions or support, email **[azerish25@gmail.com](mailto:azerish25@gmail.com)**.

## Microphone and pitch analysis

Timbre asks for microphone permission when you choose a listening or singing feature. Microphone samples are processed temporarily in memory on your device to estimate pitch, confidence and input level. Timbre does not create saved microphone recordings and does not send microphone samples to the developer, an audio-analysis server or the advertising SDK.

You can stop listening in the app and revoke microphone permission in iOS Settings. Browsing instruments and changing settings do not require microphone access.

## Settings and practice history

Timbre stores preferences and practice summaries in its local application storage. These include selected instruments and tunings, reference pitch, display preferences, session dates and duration, measured pitch-error summaries, reading counts, and vocal exercise results or demonstrated progress. They are summary values, not recordings.

The app does not operate a developer account system or a server that receives these practice records. Use **Settings → Clear practice history** to remove saved sessions and demonstrated voice progress. That action does not refund purchases or delete Apple/Google records. Removing the app removes its local application data; copies in device backups, if enabled, are governed by your device and backup settings.

## Purchases

Optional Timbre Plus purchases and restoration use Apple's App Store and StoreKit. Apple handles Apple Account authentication and payment information. Timbre receives verified transaction information needed to determine access, including the product and purchase or revocation status. Timbre does not request your payment-card details or Apple Account password. Apple's handling of App Store information is described in its [App Store privacy notice][apple-store].

## Advertising and privacy choices

A free edition may show an advertisement after an eligible completed tuning session when advertising is configured and available. Timbre Plus suppresses these ad requests. Builds without configured iOS advertising identifiers do not request ads through Timbre's advertising service.

Advertising-enabled builds integrate Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). The baseline build excludes these SDKs. When advertising and consent services are used, information may leave your device for Google and advertising providers. Depending on SDK behavior, configuration, permissions and choices, this can include an IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device or app identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, performance measurements, and crash or diagnostic information. These services use such information for advertising, measurement, service operation and related purposes; do not assume that declining personalized ads means every network request or identifier is eliminated. See Google's [iOS SDK disclosure][google-disclosure] and [privacy policy][google-privacy].

When required by the configured privacy messages and region, UMP presents privacy choices. Timbre checks the SDK's ad-request eligibility before loading an ad. **Settings → Ad privacy choices** is available when UMP requires an ongoing privacy-options entry point. Consent choices do not provide microphone recordings or local practice summaries to the ad SDK. Device-level permissions and controls, including Apple's tracking controls when applicable, are separate from these choices. [Google's UMP guidance][google-ump] explains the consent mechanism.

## Support requests and external links

If you email support, the developer receives the email address, message and any information you choose to send. Do not send passwords, payment details, private signing keys or microphone recordings unless you deliberately intend to share a particular file. Information in support correspondence is used to respond and address the reported issue; contact the address above to request deletion, subject to necessary legal or recordkeeping obligations.

Opening an external policy, support or App Store link uses that service's privacy practices. Timbre does not promise that Apple, Google, your email provider or your backup provider collects no data.

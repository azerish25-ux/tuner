## Microphone audio stays on your device

Timbre listens to your instrument or voice to measure pitch. Analysis happens on your iPhone or iPad, in memory. Timbre does not record, store or upload microphone audio. You can stop listening in the app or revoke microphone access in iOS Settings at any time.

## What Timbre stores

On your device: your settings, custom tunings, chosen instruments, lesson progress and summaries of practice sessions (date, instrument, tuning, duration and measured accuracy). Home-screen widgets and watch complications use shared or synchronized summaries such as your streak and Today activities. Clear practice history in **Settings → Clear practice history**.

**Your songs** also stores charts you enter or import, local copies of recordings you deliberately import, timing information and any Apple Music catalog links you save. Imported recordings are separate from live microphone analysis. Imported audio and full chart text stay local; optional Apple Music searches can send title and artist metadata as described below. Deleting a song removes its local library entry and imported files. Deleting the app removes its local data; device backups follow your backup settings.

## Anonymous usage analytics

When analytics is configured for the build and **Share anonymous usage data** is on, Timbre sends product events to [TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com). These help us understand, for example, how many people finish setting up, open a lesson or start a trial. An event names what happened, the app version, operating-system version, language and locale. Purchase events can include the plan, price, currency, offer type and the App Store's subscription status, such as renewal, refund or cancellation of automatic renewal.

- Timbre does not add your name, email, Apple Account, advertising identifier or precise location to analytics. It sends a hashed random installation identifier and a random session identifier so events can be grouped. We do not use these identifiers to identify you or combine events with an identity record. Hashing does not make information you deliberately type anonymous.
- Events are **never used for tracking** across other companies' apps or websites, and never sold.
- Events never contain audio, recordings or your song charts, and nothing you type, with one exception you choose to send: **Request a song** on the Songs tab sends the title you type (trimmed, at most 80 characters) and the instrument filter that was on, so the songs people ask for most can be counted. This request uses the same analytics service and installation/session identifiers described above. It is not sent while sharing is off or analytics is unavailable. The app confirms success only after the service accepts the request; if delivery fails, it lets you try again. Please don't put personal information in a request.
- Timbre also sends Apple's daily performance and stability summaries for the app (launch time, responsiveness, memory, and counts of crashes or hangs), from Apple's MetricKit, so problems can be found and fixed.
- If analytics is enabled and you installed Timbre after tapping an Apple Ads advertisement, Timbre can request Apple's AdServices attribution and include whether an ad led to the installation, campaign, ad group, keyword and conversion information in analytics. Timbre does not include attribution country or region, click dates or arbitrary attribution-response properties in these events. This does not use an advertising identifier or precise location.

You can turn sharing off at any time in Timbre's **Settings**. Queued events are discarded, active analytics and attribution HTTP requests are cancelled, and pending retries cannot start new requests under the revoked permission. An Apple AdServices token lookup that has already started cannot be interrupted, but its result cannot start a new attribution HTTP request after sharing is disabled. A request already delivered cannot be recalled. Diagnostic event names can still appear in your device's local system log; event property values, including song-request titles, are not written there by Timbre. Optional services you separately choose, such as Apple Music and support email, have their own network requests.

## Settings Timbre downloads

Timbre may download a small settings file from its developer's website, such as which subscription screen to show or which songs are free this week. Timbre does not add an installation or account identifier to that request.

To show the app's App Store rating on the subscription screen, Timbre may ask Apple's public App Store lookup service for it. Successful results are cached for about a day; failures or a storefront change can trigger another lookup. The request names the app and your storefront country; Timbre adds no installation or account identifier. As with other internet requests, the receiving service can process network information such as the IP address.

## Optional Apple Music

If you choose **Find this song on Apple Music**, Timbre asks for Apple's music authorization. The selected song's title and artist can seed a search automatically; those terms, further searches, catalog lookups and playback requests go to Apple through MusicKit. Playback uses Apple's service and may require an Apple Music subscription. Timbre does not upload imported audio or the full chart text to Apple Music. Apple's account, service and privacy settings apply to this optional feature.

## Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. Timbre receives only Apple's verified record of what you own, never your payment details. Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. See Apple's [App Store privacy notice](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-store/).

## Notifications

Notifications are created and delivered on your device. With usage sharing enabled, analytics can record whether you accepted or declined a reminder and the selected reminder time. Notification delivery itself does not use Timbre's server.

- **Practice reminders**, if you turn them on: at the time you choose, on days you haven't played yet, naming your next lesson and your streak, and a weekly summary on Sundays.
- **Trial reminder**, if you start a free trial and allow notifications: two days before the trial ends, with how much you have practised during it.

## Game Center

Game Center is disabled in the default release configuration. If a future build enables it and you sign in, Timbre can report scores, practice streaks and badges to Apple. If you agree to friends access, the app can read friends' practice streaks to show a rank and count. It does not retain their names, player IDs or friend list in Timbre's local library. Game Center is governed by Apple's privacy policy and controls.

## Sharing

When you share a card (a lesson result, your week), it is a picture of that achievement and a link to Timbre on the App Store or its website. Nothing is shared unless you choose to.

## Children

No account is required to use Timbre. Please do not include personal information in song requests. If you believe a child has sent personal information in a request or support message, contact us so we can investigate. Apple's purchase permissions and parental controls are managed through your Apple Account.

## Support and this website

If you email support, your email provider and ours process the address and message you choose to send. We use that information to respond to your request. Do not send passwords or payment details.

The privacy and support website is hosted by Cloudflare. The site includes no analytics script, advertising or account form. Cloudflare can process request information, such as IP addresses, for hosting, delivery and security under its [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). This website's hosting is separate from the optional analytics inside Timbre.

# Turkish (tr) glossary

Status: AI-reviewed, 2026-10-25. No human/native-speaker sign-off. Device and VoiceOver QA pending.

## 1. Locale decisions
- Standard Turkish (Türkiye). Consistent informal singular sen address; short singular imperatives, matching pinned tab/control names. This explicitly resolves mixed formal/informal shipped anchors. No grammatical gender; avoid unnecessary pronouns.
- Standard sentence case; proper Turkish dotted/dotless i. Quotation “…”; controls normally unquoted. Ordinary spaces, apostrophe ’, single ellipsis …, ASCII digits and unchanged literal values. Minutes dakika/dk., seconds saniye/sn., milliseconds ms; cents sent. Keep Hz, BPM, ¢, XP.
- Interface notes come from placeholders. Lesson note letters/chord symbols unchanged. Accidentals diyez / bemol / çift diyez / çift bemol. Tuner direction pes / tiz is distinct from accidentals. Never attach suffixes to an unknown placeholder; use label + colon or a neutral noun construction.
- iOS legal path Ayarlar › Apple Hesabı › Abonelikler. Feature labels: Ana Ekran, Kilitli Ekran, araç takımı, Canlı Etkinlik, Kestirmeler, Denetim Merkezi, Aile Paylaşımı. Siri, Apple Watch, Game Center unchanged. Preserve renewal, cancellation window, trial and free-tier facts fully.
- Siri commands use natural singular imperatives; Next Sonraki, Again Tekrar, Slower Daha yavaş also work as controls. Keep all ${applicationName} and other tokens.

## 3. Course and song names
Instrument titles match catalogue translations. Ear Training Kulak eğitimi, Music Theory Müzik teorisi, Rhythm Ritim, Singing Şan, Songs Şarkılar, Originals Özgün şarkılar, Standards & Classics Standartlar ve klasikler, Songbook Şarkı kitabı. Turkish ships English lessons by design; these terms apply to UI references. Established traditional titles use established Turkish titles, other titles stay English; existing localized titles take precedence. Lyrics are not translated. Names and years in credits stay unchanged. Any future translated lesson title must equal its prose/leaderboard references.

## 4. Words that mislead
Tune is akort etmek as a verb, ezgi as a noun; not the same term. Sharp/flat are diyez/bemol accidentals versus tiz/pes tuner direction. Bar is ölçü, not a UI bar; key is ton, not a keyboard key. Pick is pena or choosing (seç) by context. Lock is stable pitch confirmation, not a padlock. Take is an icra/recorded attempt. Run is a practice series or a complete passage, never running. Chart means chord diagram/sheet except statistical charts. Entonasyon means playing in tune over a drone, not guitar saddle/octave adjustment. Practice is musical çalışma. Feel means tactile vibration, not emotion. Friend challenge and final unit challenge use context, not legal dispute terms.

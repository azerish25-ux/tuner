Status: AI-reviewed, 2026-10-25. No human/native-speaker sign-off. Device and VoiceOver QA pending.

## 1. Locale decisions
- Standard Turkish (Türkiye). Consistent informal singular sen address; short singular imperatives, matching pinned tab/control names. This explicitly resolves mixed formal/informal shipped anchors. No grammatical gender; avoid unnecessary pronouns.
- Standard sentence case; proper Turkish dotted/dotless i. Quotation “…”; controls normally unquoted. Ordinary spaces, apostrophe ’, single ellipsis …, ASCII digits and unchanged literal values. Minutes dakika/dk., seconds saniye/sn., milliseconds ms; cents sent. Keep Hz, BPM, ¢, XP.
- Interface notes come from placeholders. Lesson note letters/chord symbols unchanged. Accidentals diyez / bemol / çift diyez / çift bemol. Tuner direction pes / tiz is distinct from accidentals. Never attach suffixes to an unknown placeholder; use label + colon or a neutral noun construction.
- iOS legal path Ayarlar › Apple Hesabı › Abonelikler. Feature labels: Ana Ekran, Kilitli Ekran, araç takımı, Canlı Etkinlik, Kestirmeler, Denetim Merkezi, Aile Paylaşımı. Siri, Apple Watch, Game Center unchanged. Preserve renewal, cancellation window, trial and free-tier facts fully.
- Siri commands use natural singular imperatives; Next Sonraki, Again Tekrar, Slower Daha yavaş also work as controls. Keep all ${applicationName} and other tokens.

## 4. Words that mislead
Tune is akort etmek as a verb, ezgi as a noun; not the same term. Sharp/flat are diyez/bemol accidentals versus tiz/pes tuner direction. Bar is ölçü, not a UI bar; key is ton, not a keyboard key. Pick is pena or choosing (seç) by context. Lock is stable pitch confirmation, not a padlock. Take is an icra/recorded attempt. Run is a practice series or a complete passage, never running. Chart means chord diagram/sheet except statistical charts. Entonasyon means playing in tune over a drone, not guitar saddle/octave adjustment. Practice is musical çalışma. Feel means tactile vibration, not emotion. Friend challenge and final unit challenge use context, not legal dispute terms.


## 5. Current lesson author decisions (2026-10-26)
Author: `/root/translate_turkish_lessons`. This is author guidance, not independent or native-speaker approval.
- Informal singular instructions remain consistent. Challenge lesson titles use **sınav** for a unit skill check.
- Keep note letters, chord symbols, octave digits, p-i-m-a fingering and CAGED unchanged. Translate spelled-out accidentals (G sharp → G diyez), never substitute a sign absent from source.
- Major/minor chords and scales: majör/minör. Intervals: büyük/küçük where Turkish convention requires (major third büyük üçlü; minor seventh küçük yedili). Perfect interval: tam.
- Triad: üç sesli akor; inversion: çevrim; root: kök ses; tonal home: karar sesi / karar akoru. Physical instrument bridge: köprü; nut: üst eşik. Instrument bridge and song bridge remain distinct contextual concepts.
- Numbered box: kalıp; lick: kısa cümle (numbered label Cümle); hook: ana motif; muted/chop strokes: context-specific kısa/kesik/susturulmuş vuruş. Hammer-on, pull-off, slide, bend and vibrato remain standard technique loanwords with Turkish surrounding prose.
- Note values: whole birlik, half ikilik, quarter dörtlük, eighth sekizlik, sixteenth onaltılık; duration beat: vuruş. Keep spelled counts as words and numeric counts as exact source digits.
- Up/Down shared across pitch movement and mandolin strokes: Yukarı/Aşağı (neutral direction, not tense or pitch-only wording). The first/The second refer to performed sounds: İlki/İkincisi, not screen-button order.
- Ode to Joy → Neşeye Övgü consistently. Other retained traditional and Original titles are proper names, with individual exact-source candidates for independent review. Descriptive title suffixes and credit descriptors are translated; names and years are not.
- Guitar string mnemonic E A D G B E: “Evde Akşam Dans Güzel Bir Eğlence.” This functional Turkish mnemonic preserves the initial sequence and replaces the English mnemonic, not the note letters.
- Exact-source candidates are held separately with whole-string reasons. Loanwords, notation, titles and credits are not automatically approved merely for being identical.


### 5.1 Consistency and contextual adaptations at author freeze
- Lesson interval size uses **yarım ses / tam ses** throughout (not alternating with ton). **Ton** remains musical key. Power chord is **beşli akor** throughout; power ballad is the genre loan **power balad**, not the literal “güçlü balad”.
- Course names stay **Şan**, **Viyolonsel**, **Kulak eğitimi**, **Müzik teorisi**. Lesson scales are **dizi**; the already pinned interface label Gamlar is not silently edited by this lesson task.
- Chord chart in the songbook is an **akor şeması**; chord shape is **biçim/şekil** according to the sentence. A musical bar is **ölçü**. The explicit bar/measure synonym quiz uses **ölçü / mezür**, so the question still tests two equivalent words rather than repeating the same word as question and answer.
- Movable-do solfège deliberately retains **do, re, mi, fa, sol, la, ti, do**. Do is the tonic, not invariably the absolute C pitch; ti is not silently changed to the Turkish fixed-do syllable si. Bare syllable sequences are proposed invariants with individual reasons.
- Rhythm counting localizes **and → ve**, and triplet counting **1-trip-let → 1-üç-le**, preserving three equal syllables and the source's digit. Sixteenth counting keeps the neutral e/a subdivision syllables and localizes and to ve.
- Bow frog **topuk**, tip **uç**, down-bow **çekerek yay / çekiş**, up-bow **iterek yay / itiş**; violin high/low finger positions are **yüksek/alçak**. Cello endpin **pik**, fine tuners **fiksler**. Banjo roll **roll / roll kalıbı**, forward roll **ileri roll**, pinch **çift çekiş**; clawhammer and bum-ditty remain named techniques/mnemonics.
- Dynamic **piano** is the Italian soft marking, not the instrument; **kreşendo/diminüendo** describe growing/fading volume. Staff **porte**, treble clef **sol anahtarı**, bass clef **fa anahtarı**, ledger line **ek çizgi**, enharmonic **anarmonik**.
- All arrangement section chips were included: Intro **Giriş**, Verse **Kıta**, Chorus/Refrain **Nakarat**, Bridge **Köprü**, Solo **Solo**, Outro **Çıkış**, Tag **Koda**, Variation **Çeşitleme**, Development **Gelişme**, March **Marş**, Trio **Trio**. The physical bridge has its separate **Çalgının köprüsü** source key.
- The heard-item answers **İlki / İkincisi / Üçüncüsü / Sonuncusu** name performed notes, chords or musical phrases, never answer-button locations. Bare interval answers **İkili / Üçlü / Dörtlü** differ from explicit **İkinci / Üçüncü / Dördüncü parmak**.
- Source 1700s is **1700 ile başlayan yüzyıl / 1700’de başlayan yüzyıl**: a whole century, not a decade; no extra numeric token is introduced. Source short bend is **hedefin altında kalan bend**. Violin level with floor is **yere paralel**. The fret-5 hammer-on instruction separates holding with finger 1 from picking with the other hand.

## Independently reviewed lesson overrides

- Use interval terminology and inclusive fifth/octave counting, not literal physical step counts.
- Minor seventh is küçük yedili. Harmonic arrival means resolution rather than deciding or physically stopping.
- The bar/measure quiz asks the Turkish equivalent of English bar, with Vuruş/Sus/Ölçü choices and both English synonyms explained.
- Shared banjo answer Boş beşinci tel is a neutral standalone label; the older case-specific Boş beşinci telden does not fit every role.
- Fretting, bowing, pick direction, fingernail surface and pitch register must stay explicit; song-form bridge and hardware bridge remain source-separated.

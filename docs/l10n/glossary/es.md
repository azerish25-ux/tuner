# Spanish (es) glossary

Status: AI-reviewed terminology decisions, 2026-10-25. No human or native-speaker sign-off claimed. In-device and VoiceOver verification pending.

## 1. Locale decisions
- Variant: International Spanish; Spain iOS labels, not es-MX listing labels.
- Address: tú; warm, direct teacher voice. Gender-neutral phrasing for an unknown reader. Buttons match existing table; instructions use natural imperatives. VoiceOver hints describe outcomes in the third person.
- Quotation: «…» for titles; controls unquoted; apostrophes follow existing controls, typographic ’ in new prose; single ellipsis … only.
- Spaces: ordinary single spaces; no leading/trailing spaces.
- Digits: ASCII 0–9; preserve every literal value and lesson digit group. Hz, BPM, ¢, XP, ms remain. Minute/second words follow ordinary local usage; abbreviations only when source abbreviates.
- Capitalization: sentence case; no English title case.
- Interface notes come from placeholders, never invented; lesson note letters and chord symbols remain English letter notation; never convert to solfège.
- Accidentals are distinct from tuner direction. sostenido / bemol / doble sostenido / doble bemol; direction demasiado alto / demasiado bajo.
- Legal Settings path: Ajustes › Cuenta de Apple › Suscripciones. Apple feature labels follow shipped localized strings; Siri, Apple Watch, Game Center remain unchanged. Retain all subscription facts, cancellation windows and free-tier qualifications.
- Siri: natural spoken imperative, all ${applicationName} and other tokens intact; Next/Again/Slower use one shared control/command translation.

## 4. Words that mislead
Tune as a verb means tuning, as a noun a melody. Sharp/flat on the tuner mean too high/low, not accidentals. Bar means measure; key means tonality. Pick means plectrum or plucking in music, choose in UI. Lock means a stable in-tune confirmation, not a padlock. Take is one recorded performance, not taking a lesson. Run means a practice streak or an uninterrupted passage, not running. Chart means an instrumental chord diagram/sheet unless progress statistics explicitly demand a graph. Intonation is playing in tune over a drone, never octave/saddle adjustment. Practice is musical practice. Feel is haptic vibration, not emotion. A challenge to a friend differs from the unit's final challenge lesson; preserve context.

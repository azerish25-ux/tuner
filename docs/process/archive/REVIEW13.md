# REVIEW 13: Timbre (iOS), independent jury pass

**Overall: 8.7 / 10.** Timbre is the best-built tuner in its category, and its lesson app is far beyond GuitarTuna's. A 9.5 needs three more things. The hero frames need to be clean. Teaching has to be *shown* as well as described. The song catalogue has to look adult-first. Today an Apple Design Award juror would shortlist it, and an editor would hesitate over the frames below.

## 1. Scores
- **Visual design & polish: 8.7.** One confident system: Sora type, mint and amber on near-black, a Liquid Glass tab bar, studio-lit RealityKit headstocks and generative sleeves. Held back by seams in hero moments (results ring, song-sheet band), monotone teal card stacks, and iPad pages with dead space (Tools grid, tuner readout).
- **Motion/delight: 8.8.** Spring tokens (Theme.swift:217), 4 Metal shaders (strobe ripple and lamp, heat star, lock shimmer), zoom transitions, stars landing and Reduce Motion handled throughout. The drone and ear-training stages are the static exceptions.
- **Tuner: 9.4.** Best in the category: polyphonic strum-to-tune ("Tune A2 up 12¢ first"), strobe, 3D peg cues, string-health drift, intonation, 60 instruments and 102 tunings. Only the iPad composition and the idle copy lag behind.
- **Lessons & curriculum: 8.3.** 557 listening lessons, a highway with lyrics, engraved notation and 153 full arrangements. Against that: no demonstration of hands, synthesized ukulele, mandolin and banjo tones, 96 guitar lessons against Yousician's and Fender's depth, and nursery tunes at the front of the song shelves.
- **Retention/habit loop: 9.0.** At Duolingo's level: a Today set, streak freezes, quests, XP and levels, 42 badges, spaced review, coach notes, a reminder ask at the peak moment, a Live Activity and friend streaks.
- **Monetization/paywall: 8.6.** Contextual paywalls (Tanpura, tools), a trial timeline with a day-12 reminder, win-back and exit offers, offer codes and lifetime. But the onboarding paywall, which gets the most traffic, sells with one bullet and a small piece of art.
- **Platform integration: 9.3.** Widgets, Control Center controls, a Live Activity, a Watch app and complication, Shortcuts and Spotlight, TipKit, Game Center and multi-column iPad layouts.
- **Store readiness: 8.2.** The listing, keywords, age-rating audit and preview workflow are thorough. But store frames #1 and #6 carry visible defects, #2 sells "0 0 0" open strings, iPad #2 is a sheet over the tuner, and the privacy URL is still TODO.

## 2. What stands between this and 9.5 (ranked)
1. **Clean store set (S).** Screens 10b, 62, 60 and ipad-landscape-instruments. Fix bugs 1 and 2 below. Recapture #2 as *Kite Weather* on the highway mid-chorus, with the lyric row, fret numbers and a combo of 8 or more (TimbreUITests.swift:496). Shoot iPad #2 as a full-page browser. The user would see a first row of three frames, each selling a moment, with no artifacts.
2. **Adult-first Songs shelves (S).** Screens 63 and ipad-*-songs. SongsView.swift:1388-1391 orders "3 chords or fewer" easiest first, so it opens on Mary Had a Little Lamb and She'll Be Coming 'Round the Mountain, followed by Twinkle, Old MacDonald and Yankee Doodle. Rank Originals and Standards first (Porchlight, Down by the Riverside, Loch Lomond…) and move nursery tunes into a lower "Campfire & kids" shelf. A $35-a-year adult would then see a modern catalogue.
3. **Onboarding paywall that shows the goods (M).** Screen 06. PaywallPlan.swift:210-214 gives one outcome line, and the hero sleeve is about 90 pt. Put a slowly drifting rail of 6–8 real sleeves from the user's plan behind the title. Add a proof line, "557 lessons · 153 songs · 60 instruments", and two more outcomes: every instrument, and ear games and tools.
4. **Placement framed as a gain (S).** Screen 05b. OnboardingView.swift:506-516 shows a lock and "Revisit them any time with Timbre Pro" one screen before the paywall. Change it to "You start at Unit 7 — skip what you already know" and leave Pro to the compare table.
5. **"Show me" demonstrations (L).** Screens 51, 52b and 53. Explain steps are a static chart and a paragraph. Use the shipped usdz neck rigs (Resources/Models) to show fingertips landing on the 3D fretboard and a pick sweeping across the strings. This is the closest code-only answer to the teacher video in Fender Play and Simply Guitar.
6. **Strum direction that matches the arrow (S).** Screens 53–55. LessonMotion.swift:66-73 uses the default Chart view, where the "↓" down-strum pick travels *up* the screen. Default the rhythm and strum steps to Player view, so ↓ falls down the screen. Keep Chart for fretting steps.
7. **iPad tuner composition (M).** Screens ipad-portrait-tune and ipad-landscape-tune. Readout.swift:263 caps the note at 112 pt, so on a 13" screen D3 floats small under a headstock cropped at the right edge. Scale the note to about 190 pt and the meter to the full column through TuneArrangement, and centre the stage in portrait.
8. **iPad Play tools grid (M).** Screen ipad-*-practice. PracticeView.swift:314-336 draws six big tiles, each with a 20-pt glyph and a label, and they read as placeholders. Give each tile a live mini preview: a pendulum at the last BPM, the drone dial, the last chord box, scale dots, a harmonic node and a voice trace.
9. **Drone and ear training that look alive (S/M).** In 42 the running drone looks the same as idle (41) apart from the button. Add a breathing halo and vibrating strings, and start Listen automatically (DroneView.swift:159). In 23 the question card is one bar and a "?" in a 400-pt box. Animate both notes rising to their pitch, using the staircase art from the Play hub (EarTrainingView.swift).
10. **Stars that mean something (S).** Screens 61→62. A 50 % "Keep going" play-along still ends in "Great work" with three stars, plus a coach note that a move didn't land. Let the final play-along gate the third star, or show per-step scores (LessonResults.swift:54-59, Coach).
11. **Sleeve variety (S).** Screens 63, 64, 06 and 16. One sunset/rays motif covers Song of the Day (Kite Weather), the paywall sleeve (Greensleeves) and Yankee Doodle. Never repeat a motif within a view or on a hero, and add more motif families (SongCover.swift:20-23).
12. **Tuner idle state (S).** Screens 49, 49b, 49c and 90. "— · 196.00 Hz" (Readout.swift:327) reads as a glitch, and "Play G3" appears twice (on the peg chip and in the status line). Show "Target 196.00 Hz" until a pitch is heard, and keep a single "Play G3".
13. **Copy polish (S).** 32: "Average ¢ off" (JourneyView.swift:222) should read "Avg. cents off". 20: the Today ring "9 min" should read "9 min left". 64: "Words: Timbre" (SongOpener.swift:328) should read "Words & music · Timbre Originals".
14. **A stable Today plan (S).** Today's tool is chosen from the tuner's *current* instrument (Streaks.swift:97-124): 20 lists Ear training and 50 lists Scale workout. Switching instruments reshuffles a plan the user has already seen. Pin the day's set once it has been shown.

**Outside assets / owner decisions**
- Multi-velocity CC0 or commissioned recordings: flat-top acoustic with strums, ukulele, mandolin and banjo. Today guitar is a one-velocity archtop, and ukulele, mandolin and banjo are ToneSynth.
- Produced stems (live drums, bass and keys) for the 50 Originals' "Preview with band"; commissioned hand art or teacher video for Unit 1.
- A hosted privacy-policy URL. A pricing test: lifetime at 2.6× the yearly price risks cannibalising the annual plan.
- Pre-launch social proof (TestFlight quotes, teacher endorsements) for the paywall and the listing.

## 3. Bugs
1. **62, store #6: the ring behind the stars is cut flat at the top.** SparkBurst (320 pt) sits in a 136-pt frame inside a ScrollView that clips it (LessonResults.swift:89-95). Fix by padding the top, or by drawing the burst in an overlay outside the ScrollView.
2. **10b, store #1: "✓ In tune · 0¢" badge and an "In tune" status line show together.** TuneView.swift:321-335 hides the line on `inTune && audio.running && !preserved`, while NoteReadout shows its badge from its own lock state (Readout.swift:288-296). Drive both from one flag.
3. **64: a hard horizontal seam at the close button in the song-sheet hero.** The CoverMesh backdrop (SongOpener.swift:125-131) starts below LessonPlayer's top bar (LessonPlayer.swift:74-78), leaving a flat teal band above it. Draw it in LessonPlayer's ZStack while `opening`.
4. **ipad-landscape-instruments, iPad store #2.** It is a sheet over the live tuner: AUTO, MANUAL, REFERENCE and Strum/Pause show at the edges, and the bottom row of tiles is cut flush.
5. **49, 49b, 49c, 90.** The "— · 196.00 Hz" placeholder dash (Readout.swift:327).
6. **52 → 52b.** "Step 2 of 5" shows on two consecutive screens, because the tune check borrows the next step's number, so the progress bar does not advance (LessonPlayer top bar).
7. **53–55.** The "↓ 6→1" key sits over a pick that travels up the screen (item 6).

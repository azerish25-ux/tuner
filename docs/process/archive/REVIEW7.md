## 2. Highest-impact changes (ranked)
1. **(M) The tuner hero shows which peg to turn** (10, 10b, 11 vs 07, 14, 90). In steady state the 3D guitar sits small at full body, with no peg ring or turn arrow, and the selected D3 (11) isn't marked on the model. Keep the headstock close-up with the peg ring and ↻ whenever a string is out of tune or selected. Pull back to the whole instrument only for the in-tune celebration. Check `focus: audio.running && !store.referenceMode` (Tune/TuneView.swift:163) and `target` (Stage/InstrumentStage.swift:182). This is App Store shot #1.
2. **(L) A highway worth an App Preview** (59, 60, 61). Highway.swift:769 sets `lookahead = 2.8` s, so a 60 BPM take shows one small gem. Set the window in beats (2 bars or more) so 3–4 gems are visible. Make gems about 1.5× (at least 70% of lane width), with the note name under the fret. Add a "Perfect / Late" pop on the hit line and a combo counter in the HUD. On 61, fill the empty card with a per-note timing strip (one dot per note, offset early or late). Show scores under 50% in amber with a retry framing, not a red "42%".
3. **(M) One paywall timeline, plus a real hero** (06, 16, 40). Merge `milestoneStrip` and `trialTimeline` (Paywall/PaywallView.swift:156–160) into one track: Today, the first lesson, Day 7 chords, Day 12 "we remind you", Day 14 billing. That frees about 70 pt for a full-width PaywallPreview; the guitar thumbnail currently collides with the runway and the TIMBRE PRO pill. Make the first bullet match the paywall's reason. On 40 the headline is the drone, but the bullets are about songs and lessons (PaywallPlan.swift:120).
4. **(S) Make the reminder opt-in visible** (62). The "Make it a habit" button sits under the pinned "Next lesson", and only a mint sliver shows (Learn/LessonResults.swift:90, :217). Make it one row with an inline "Remind me · 7 PM" pill, placed above the XP tiles. It is the main lever for retention on days 1–7.
5. **(M) Moments to share** (62, 32). The only ShareLink is in Tune/Celebration.swift. Add three things. An ImageRenderer share card on the results screen (stars, accuracy, streak). A full-screen celebration at 3, 7 and 30 days, with the flame (Design/GameUI.swift). A Sunday "Your week" recap card on Progress (minutes, best accuracy, strings kept in tune).
6. **(M) Explain and chord steps that move** (06b, 51, 52b). Explain steps are 741 of 3,478 steps. They show a static diagram, with a decorative guitar photo filling the bottom third. Animate the fingering: dots drop in finger order as each note sounds, with "Hear it" beside the diagram. Add a large count-in "1 2 3 4", and label a red beat with the reason ("early 120 ms") (LessonSteps.swift `RhythmStep` :439, LessonMotion.swift).
8. **(M) Songs that sell** (63). A level-5 player on a 7-day streak gets "Mary Had a Little Lamb" as Song of the Day. Pick it by level from unfinished Originals instead. The covers are two-letter monograms (ML, KW, Po). Generate procedural art in Songs/SongCover.swift: genre palette, shape or waveform motif, title in Sora. Add a play-preview button on the cover.

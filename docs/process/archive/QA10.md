# QA10: pre-release code audit (reading only), branch v2/foundations @ 55f87c1

Scope: the last ~40 commits, mainly Tune/Strum*, Learn (Song*, Highway, LessonPlayer, LessonExplain, LessonSteps), Practice (Drone*, PlayHub*, Chords, Scales, Metronome), Journey/Coach*, Intents/*, TimbreWidgets/*, Paywall/*, Services (AppStore, CommerceService, LiveActivity, WidgetBridge), Audio/*, and LocalStore.

## Critical

### C1. The screen auto-locks during tuning, play-alongs and song finales, and locking kills the microphone
- **Where:** No `isIdleTimerDisabled` anywhere in the app (grep returns nothing). `Audio/AudioService.swift:70-73` stops capture on `didEnterBackgroundNotification`.
- **Failure:** The player's hands are on the instrument, so nothing touches the screen. With Low Power Mode (Auto-Lock forced to 30 s), 340 of the curriculum's timed takes run 25 s or longer. With the default 1-minute Auto-Lock, 21 takes do, including `song-silent-night` at 112 s and `song-greensleeves` at 87 s. Hands-free tuning (a 12-string, a cello) is also affected. The screen dims and locks, the app goes to the background and `stopCapture()` runs. The take carries on with no microphone, so every remaining note is graded as a miss. In lessons the click keeps playing (see H2). The tuner simply stops.
- **Fix:** Set `UIApplication.shared.isIdleTimerDisabled = true` while `audio.running || audio.metronomeRunning` (or while a lesson or the tuner is in front). Reset it to `false` on stop and on background. A single `.onChange(of: audio.running)` in RootView is enough.

## High

### H1. A listen or quiz step's tone sequence keeps playing into the next step and switches off that step's microphone
- **Where:** `Learn/LessonPlayer.swift:593-603` (`ChoiceStep.play`: an unstored `Task` that loops over `sound` groups, 0.8–1.35 s each). It starts on appear (`:460`) and again on each "Hear it" tap (`:519`). Nothing cancels it on disappear. `Audio/AudioService.swift:266-268` (`playTones` does `if running { stopCapture() }` and never resumes).
- **Failure:** Listen steps hold up to 12 groups, so 10–16 s of audio. The learner recognises the answer, taps it, then Continue.
  - If the next step is a `NotesStep` (78 listen → notes transitions in the curriculum), it starts listening on appear (`LessonSteps.swift:117`). The stale task's next `playTones` then stops capture. The step sits at "Listening…" for good: nothing restarts the microphone until "Hear it" or "I played it".
  - If the next step is another listen step (141 transitions), the two questions' notes interleave, and the new question's tones are cut off by the old ones. That corrupts ear-training answers.
  - Tapping "Hear it" twice also runs two sequences at once.
- **Fix:** Keep the task in `@State var playTask`. Cancel it in `play()` before starting a new one and in `.onDisappear`, and check `Task.isCancelled` after each sleep. Alternatively, send the whole sequence to `AudioService` as one phrase, as EarTrainingView does.

### H2. A lesson's click keeps playing in the background indefinitely
- **Where:** `Audio/AudioService.swift:70-73` (background stops only the band and the microphone: "metronome and drone keep playing"). Lessons start the shared metronome at `Learn/Highway.swift:642`, `Learn/LessonSteps.swift:837` and `Learn/SongPerform.swift:332`. Their tick/expire loops (`Highway.swift:661-669`, `LessonSteps.swift:873-881`, `SongPerform.swift:354-360`) are driven by `TimelineView` and freeze in the background. `UIBackgroundModes: [audio]` keeps the process alive. `Services/LiveActivityController.swift:263-266` treats a lesson click as "not sounding".
- **Failure:** The player locks the phone mid-take (or C1 locks it for them), or switches app. The click keeps sounding from the pocket until they come back. On return the take is closed out as all misses, and that junk take feeds `CoachTakes` ("your notes sat 400 ms late…").
- **Fix:** On background, stop the metronome unless the Metronome tool owns it (`LiveActivityController.metronomeClaimed`). Better: have the lesson steps observe `scenePhase` and call their own stop/reset (`stopTake()` / `reset()`).

### H3. The Next song widget marks a paying user's songs "PRO" or locked after every cold launch
- **Where:**
  - `Services/AppStore.swift:102` runs `publishWidgets()` inside `init`. Line `:120` calls `WidgetLibrary.publish(self)`.
  - `Intents/WidgetLibraryPublisher.swift:72-73, 126-132` compute each song's access through `store.canPlay`, which reads `commerce.isPro`.
  - `Services/CommerceService.swift:10, 35`: `isPro` stays `false` until an async entitlement refresh finishes.
  - `Root/RootView.swift:69`: the `isPro` change is only logged; nothing republishes.
  - `TimbreWidgets/NextSongWidget.swift:213-221` draws the badge.
- **Failure:** Each launch writes a library where every non-free song is `"pro"` or `"locked"` and reloads the widget timelines. The widget then shows a gold PRO badge or a lock on content the user has paid for. Because `SongOfTheDay.pick` weighs access, it can also feature a different song from the in-app hero. This lasts until the next practice record is saved.
- **Fix:** Call `store.publishWidgets()` from `.onChange(of: commerce.isPro)` (and once `entitlementResolved` turns true). Skip `WidgetLibrary.publish` in `init` until entitlements have resolved.

### H4. `SparkBurst` redraws at display rate forever, on the Play hub and on most lesson screens
- **Where:** `Learn/LessonPlayer.swift:376`: `TimelineView(.animation(paused: reduceMotion))`. It pauses only under Reduce Motion; `draw` returns early once `t ≥ 0.9`, but the timeline keeps ticking. The view is always mounted at:
  - `Practice/DailySetCard.swift:253` (Play hub Today ring)
  - `LessonPlayer.swift:571` (the correct answer of every quiz and listen step)
  - `LessonExplain.swift:239`
  - `LessonSteps.swift:167, 403, 1066`
  - `EarTrainingView.swift:186, 331`
  - `LessonResults.swift:87`, `SongStoryCard.swift:104`, `PlacementCheckView.swift:223`, `StreakCelebration.swift:70`
- **Failure:** While any of these screens is up, SwiftUI re-evaluates the timeline and redraws an empty Canvas every frame. On ProMotion devices that holds the display at 120 Hz. This is a steady battery cost on the screens people spend the most time on, for an effect that lasts 0.9 s.
- **Fix:** Track `@State var active` that turns true when `trigger` changes and false after 0.9 s, and use `paused: reduceMotion || !active`. Or mount the burst only while it is playing.

## Medium

### M1. Delayed tasks in note steps reopen the lesson microphone after the lesson has closed, leaving the tuner dead
- **Where:**
  - `Learn/LessonSteps.swift:269-279` (`NotesStep.hear()`): an unstored 1.1 s `Task` calls `listen()` if `!finished`. `onDisappear` (`:118`) cannot stop it.
  - `LessonSteps.swift:1149-1155` (`TuneCheckStep.lock()`): the 450 ms task sets `store.gameTargetHz` after the view has gone.
  - `Services/AppStore.swift:635-651, 481-485, 973-987`.
- **Failure:**
  - **"Hear it" then quick close.** The player taps "Hear it" on a note step, then X → Leave within about 1 s. `LessonPlayer.onDisappear` clears the listener, then the stale task runs `startLessonListening(owner:)`. The microphone stays on with a dead `lessonListener`, so every reading goes there. The Tune tab shows "Play E2" and never reacts; pause and resume do not help, because neither clears `lessonListener`. Strum does not open either, because `beginStrum` requires `lessonOwner == nil`. This lasts until another lesson is opened or the app is relaunched.
  - **Tune check then quick close.** Same pattern in the tune check: `gameTargetHz` is left set. `targetHz` (`AppStore.swift:170`) then measures against a lesson string while the screen names the selected string. `measuringTuning` is false, so nothing locks or saves.
- **Fix:** Keep both tasks in `@State` and cancel them in `onDisappear`, as `ChordStep` already does with `hearTask`. In `startListening()`/`toggleListening()`, clear a `lessonListener` whose owner is not on screen.

### M2. Lessons take over the Metronome tool, play off its click, then silently stop it
- **Where:**
  - `Audio/AudioService.swift:706-725`: `setMetronome` reuses a running renderer, so `bar`, `metronomeFrames` and the clock are not reset.
  - `AudioService.swift:424-429`: `heardStart` gives up when |shift| ≥ 0.75 s.
  - `Learn/LessonPlayer.swift:88` (unconditional `stopMetronome()` on close), `Highway.swift:171, 642`, `LessonSteps.swift:683, 837`, `SongPerform.swift:98, 332`.
  - `Services/AppStore.swift:914-917`.
- **Failure:** The user leaves the Metronome tool clicking (it keeps going, with a Live Activity) and opens a lesson.
  - The play-along keeps the old click's phase. The count-in, runway and judging assume a click that starts at the tap, so grading is off by up to a beat, and the band's bar counter is wrong.
  - Closing any lesson stops the user's metronome.
  - `practiceStarted["rhythm"]` is never finished, so that rhythm session never reaches the streak or history.
- **Fix:** In the lessons' `start()`, call `audio.stopMetronome()` first to force a fresh count. On close, stop the click only if the lesson started it (`!LiveActivityController.metronomeClaimed`), or restore the tool's settings.

### M3. Every settings change rewrites and re-reads the whole state file on the main thread, once per slider step
- **Where:**
  - `Sources/TimbreCore/Persistence/LocalStore.swift:144-153`: `persist` validates every record, JSON-encodes the full state, does an atomic write, reads the file back, decodes it and compares.
  - `Services/AppStore.swift:262-266, 895-904`.
  - `Practice/MetronomeView.swift:196`: the BPM `Slider` calls `store.setMetronome(bpm:)` on every step.
  - Records are never trimmed (`LocalStore.swift:130-137`).
  - Each `save()` also rebuilds the whole widget library (`AppStore.swift:578`, `WidgetLibraryPublisher.swift:59-107`).
- **Failure:** Dragging the tempo slider performs up to about 270 full-file round trips on the main thread. The file grows with history: about 5 records a day plus 600 string samples. Each change also republishes `settings`, which re-renders every tab root (see M15), so the slider stutters. Finishing a lesson performs 4–5 round trips while the results screen animates.
- **Fix:**
  - Commit BPM `onEditingChanged(false)` and keep the live value in `@State`.
  - In `persist`, drop the read-back decode or move it off the main thread.
  - Debounce or coalesce `updateSettings` writes.
  - Cap or roll up old `records`.

### M4. The streak widget still says "Practised today" after midnight and keeps a broken streak
- **Where:**
  - `TimbreWidgets/TimbreWidgets.swift:38-41`: the timeline reloads just after midnight but re-reads the same snapshot.
  - `TimbreWidgets.swift:177-200`: `StreakView` shows the raw `snapshot.practicedToday`, `streak` and `week`.
  - The ageing logic (`StreakStatus`, `Intents/StreakSnippet.swift:7-27`) is not in the widget target (`project.yml` TimbreWidgets sources).
- **Failure:** The user practised yesterday and has not opened the app today. The widget shows a lit flame and "Practised today", and the 7-day bars do not shift. After a missed day it still shows the old streak. The comment's intent ("Redraw just after midnight so practised today resets") is not met.
- **Fix:** Add `Intents/StreakSnippet.swift` (or just `StreakStatus`) to the widget target and render `StreakStatus(entry.snapshot, now: entry.date)`.

### M5. An expired subscription stays Pro until the app is killed
- **Where:** `Services/CommerceService.swift:25-36, 111-141`. Entitlements refresh only at init, on `Transaction.updates`, on paywall load and on restore. `Root/RootView.swift:64-67` has no refresh when the app comes to the foreground.
- **Failure:** An expiry without renewal produces no transaction update. A suspended app (common for days) keeps `isPro == true`, which unlocks Pro lessons, instruments and the drone. Pro users never see the paywall, so its refresh never runs either.
- **Fix:** Call `await commerce.refreshEntitlements()` when the app becomes active. Also schedule a one-shot refresh at `renewalDate`.

### M6. A declined Ask to Buy leaves the purchase button disabled for the rest of the session
- **Where:** `Services/CommerceService.swift:88-89` sets `pending = true`. `pending` is cleared only when a purchase starts or a verified `Transaction.updates` arrives (`:31`). `Paywall/PaywallView.swift:1131` disables the button while `commerce.pending`.
- **Failure:** A parent declines, so no transaction ever arrives. Every paywall stays on "Waiting for approval" with a disabled CTA until relaunch. Restore does not clear it either.
- **Fix:** Clear `pending` when the paywall appears (`load()`) and in `restore()`. Or keep the button enabled and let StoreKit handle a repeat request.

### M7. A full tune by strum counts the same minutes twice
- **Where:**
  - `Services/AppStore.swift:1005-1009`: `completeStrumTune` saves a record from `sessionStartedUptime` when `sessionCount < 3`, without resetting the session.
  - `AppStore.swift:566-574`: `endTuningSession` later saves a second record from the same start.
  - `AppStore.swift:439-443`: `startListening` keeps the old `sessionStarted`.
- **Failure:** Open Strum and get every string in tune in one strum (record 1, covering start to now). Back on the tuner a few readings push `sessionCount` to 3 or more. On a tab switch, record 2 covers start to later, overlapping record 1. `totalMinutes`, the week recap, the coach's "more time" note and the minutes achievement are all inflated.
- **Fix:** In `completeStrumTune`, call `resetTuningMeasurements()` after saving, keeping `lockedCourses`. Or always let `endTuningSession` own the record.

### M8. The root paywall is not modal for VoiceOver
- **Where:** `Root/RootView.swift:31-35` draws the paywall as an `.overlay` over the TabView. `PaywallView` sets no `.accessibilityAddTraits(.isModal)` (only `Celebration.swift:76` and `StreakCelebration.swift:49` do).
- **Failure:** VoiceOver swipes past the paywall into the tuner and the tab bar behind it, so the user can activate hidden controls such as the listen button or a tab while the paywall covers them.
- **Fix:** Add `.accessibilityAddTraits(.isModal)` to the PaywallView root (and `.accessibilityHidden(store.showPaywall)` on the TabView content).

### M9. The paywall's price and terms truncate at large text sizes
- **Where:** `Paywall/PaywallView.swift:1104-1107` (`terms`: "Free for N days, then $X a year.", `.lineLimit(2).minimumScaleFactor(0.85)`, in the pinned bottom bar) and `:1127` (CTA `.lineLimit(1).minimumScaleFactor(0.7)`).
- **Failure:** At AX3 and above, or in long locales, the after-trial price ends in "…" and the CTA is cut off. This is an accessibility defect, and App Review guideline 3.1.2 requires the renewal price to be clearly shown.
- **Fix:** Remove the line limits at accessibility sizes (`typeSize.isAccessibilitySize ? nil : 2`) and let the bar grow, or move the terms into the scroll content when they do not fit.

### M10. On the strum screen, VoiceOver numbers the strings differently from the visible chips
- **Where:** `Tune/StrumModel.swift:381-384` (`StrumWords.name` reads "String \(meter.id)", the course index, so the low E is 1). The chip shows `meter.number` from the anchor, so the low E is 6 (`StrumModel.swift:243-246, 300-304`; `StrumMeters.swift:245`). Also used at `StrumFinale.swift:36, 177`.
- **Failure:** VoiceOver says "String 1, E2" for the lane whose chip reads "6". Lessons call the thinnest string "String 1" (`Highway.swift:318`), so blind players are pointed at the wrong string.
- **Fix:** Use `meter.number ?? meter.id`, and for pairs the anchors' numbers.

### M11. Share cards are rendered on the main thread just as the celebration animations start
- **Where:**
  - `Tune/Celebration.swift:80-95`: `renderCard()` runs synchronously inside `onChange(of: celebrate)`, as the burst and card spring in.
  - `Learn/SongStoryCard.swift:97, 263-265`: a 1080×1920 poster is rendered before the finale reveal, during the take → finale transition.
  - `Learn/LessonResults.swift:554`: `.task` rendering while stars and XP animate.
  - `Journey/WeekRecap.swift:79-81`.
- **Failure:** `ImageRenderer` at 3× blocks the main thread for tens of milliseconds, so the reward moments hitch visibly.
- **Fix:** Render after the entrance (sleep about 0.8 s inside the `.task`) or only when Share is tapped (`Transferable` with lazy rendering). Keep the first frames free of the render.

### M12. The Tune tab redraws at full display rate even when it is not listening
- **Where:** All of these run without a pause condition:
  - `Tune/TuneView.swift:250`: the readout's `TimelineView(.animation)`.
  - `Stage/StudioStage.swift:88`: "every frame".
  - `Stage/PegCue.swift:100`.
  - `Tune/ChromaticStage.swift:14`.

  Related in lessons:
  - `LessonSteps.swift:663`: the progression strip's `.periodic(by: 0.1)` timeline runs as long as the step is on screen.
  - `LessonSteps.swift:773`: the strum sweep keeps a full-rate timeline in the `.done` phase, where it draws a still picture.
- **Failure:** The tuner is the home tab. Paused, or with the microphone denied, it still evaluates several bodies and Canvases per frame (on top of RealityKit), draining battery while idle.
- **Fix:** Pause them when nothing moves. For example: `paused: !audio.running && !store.live.preserved && motion.cents == nil`; for the stage, pause once the dolly has settled; pause the strum sweep when `phase == .done`.

### M13. Siri, Spotlight and widget links are dropped or misrouted while a lesson is open
- **Where:**
  - `Intents/DeepLink.swift:56-59`: it closes only Settings and the picker, not a lesson cover.
  - `DeepLink.swift:129-150`.
  - `Learn/LearnView.swift:84, 89`: `courseId` is switched, then `guard playing == nil else { return }` drops the link.
  - `Tune/TuneView.swift:63`: `autoListen` runs on appear.
  - `Services/AppStore.swift:639`: `guard !audio.running`.
- **Failure:** With a lesson open, "Open lesson X" or a Next song tap does nothing visible, while the Learn course changes underneath. A Quick tune or Tune link changes the tab under the lesson cover; TuneView appears and can start the tuner's microphone (with the tuner's range) under the lesson, and later lesson steps reuse that range. When the open lesson came from Songs or Progress, LearnView tries to present a second full-screen cover after its 2 s wait, which UIKit rejects.
- **Fix:** In `DeepLink.open`, close any presented lesson first (a shared "dismiss covers" flag the players observe), or queue the link until the cover closes. Don't change `courseId` when the link is going to be dropped.

### M14. Looping a bar at the slowest rung runs the click at 30 BPM while the runway runs at 29 BPM
- **Where:**
  - `Learn/Highway.swift:608`: loop BPM is `max(20, (takeTempo * 0.75).rounded())`.
  - `Audio/AudioService.swift:713`: the click is clamped to ≥ 30.
  - `Sources/TimbreCore/Learn/LessonEngine.swift:135-138`: the ladder's floor is 20.
- **Failure:** `man-3-3` (50 BPM) at the 75 % rung runs at 38 BPM. Tapping a missed note to loop its bar gives 29 BPM on the runway and judge, but 30 on the click. After 4 passes the notes are about 1 s off the click, so the practice loop marks good playing as late or missed.
- **Fix:** Clamp the loop and ladder tempos to 30 (the renderer's floor), or let the renderer accept 20–300.

### M15. Learn and Songs recompute lesson access with quadratic work on every store change
- **Where:**
  - `Sources/TimbreCore/Learn/LessonEngine.swift:394-398`: `isUnlocked` rebuilds `course.lessons`, a `flatMap` (`Curriculum.swift:46`), on every call.
  - `Learn/LearnView.swift:313-316`: called for each of about 96 nodes, in a non-lazy VStack.
  - `Songs/SongsView.swift:81-87, 191-211, 218-235, 266-278`: about 105 songs, each through `LessonAccess.of` plus `isUnlocked` several times per body, plus sorting `finishedDates` and linear `curriculum.lesson(id:)` scans.
- **Failure:** Every `@Published` change on AppStore re-runs these bodies for each tab root that is live: a settings save, the reward pulse, every BPM slider step (M3), every chromatic note change (`referenceMidi`). That is tens of thousands of array copies per change, and it causes visible jank on older devices.
- **Fix:** Cache each course's lesson index (`[lessonId: position]`) and `lessons` array once in `Curriculum`. Memoise each song's access per `(learn, isPro)` in a small view model, not in `body`.

### M16. The play-along runway builds new text at a new font size for every gem, every frame
- **Where:** `Learn/Highway.swift:1517, 1550-1559, 1563-1575`: `Text(...).font(.system(size: 25 * sc …))`, with `sc` changing every frame, drawn per visible gem along with the note name and finger tag. There are also per-frame `Color.mix` calls.
- **Failure:** Each frame forces a new font instance and text layout for 10+ gems at 60–120 Hz during the core lesson screen, which risks dropped frames exactly where timing is judged.
- **Fix:** Resolve each label once at a fixed size (`context.resolve(Text)`, cached per fret and finger) and scale it with `context.scaleBy`. Precompute the mixed tints per string.

## Checked and clean (no finding)
- **Persistence decoding:** all persisted structs decode older saves (`UserSettings` v1 fields are unchanged; `LearnProgress` uses `decodeIfPresent`, with `try?` for weak spots).
- **Validation limits:** none were tightened in the last 40 commits.
- **Curriculum data:** no zero BPM, empty notes or patterns, chord/bar mismatches or out-of-range answers. Every song ends on a take.
- **DSP preconditions:** PolyTuner, ChordRecognizer, OnsetDetector and MetronomePattern are guarded by callers (sample rate ≥ 8 kHz, tempo clamped).
- **Pro gating through intents, deep links, Spotlight, widgets, the Daily set and coach actions:** every path ends in `canPlay`, `canTune` or an `isPro` check (`DeepLink.openDrone` and `openLesson`, `LearnView.open`, `PracticeView.tool`).
- **The shared `audio.frames` slot:** strum ↔ tuner ↔ lessons hand it over correctly (owner checks, `endStrum` and `stopLessonListening` clear it). The only leak found is M1's stale tasks.
- **StoreKit:** verified transactions are finished on both purchase and updates. The reminders (`PracticeReminder`, `TrialReminder`) use fixed identifiers, so nothing is scheduled twice.
- **Reduce Motion:** respected by the phase and keyframe animators, the embers, the motes, the flame and the paywall scenes.

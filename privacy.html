Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026.

Microphone audio stays on your device

Timbre listens to your instrument or voice to measure pitch. Analysis happens on your iPhone or iPad. Timbre does not record, store or upload microphone audio.

What Timbre stores

On your device only: your settings, custom tunings, the instruments you choose, and summaries of practice sessions (date, instrument, tuning, duration and measured accuracy). Home-screen widgets read your practice streak from storage shared between the app and its widgets on the same device. You can clear this history at any time in Settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. Timbre receives only Apple's verified record of what you own, never your payment details.

Notifications

If you start a free trial and allow notifications, Timbre schedules a local reminder before the trial ends. It is created on your device.

Analytics

This version keeps product events on your device. If anonymous usage analytics are added in a future version, this page and the App Store privacy label will be updated first. Timbre does not sell data or use it for advertising.

Children

Timbre does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
